Agayha Studio

House, techno and a night of music in Misakicho, Ishigaki.

We are a DJ bar and cultural space on the third floor of Ocean Building. Locals, travelers and solo visitors are welcome to enjoy dance music through our sound system.

About a five-minute walk from Ishigaki Port Remote Island Terminal. Look for Ocean Building next to Shineicho Park, then head to the third floor.
Music & drinks
House, techno and other dance music, with cocktails, Japanese liqueurs and Ishigaki awamori.

DJ events & tonight's plans

We host DJ events, including on Saturdays. The latest dates, lineups, opening changes and event admission details are announced on our official Instagram @agayha_3f.

If you are visiting Ishigaki and would like to DJ or host an event, contact us to discuss the details.

DJ practice & space rental

Daytime space rental is available between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM. DJ practice is ¥1,500 for the first hour and ¥1,000 for each additional hour. Venue rental is ¥2,500 for the first hour and ¥1,500 for each additional hour.

Contact us on LINE or call to discuss availability and your plans.

Before you arrive

Can I visit on my own?

Yes. Solo visitors, local guests and travelers are welcome.

Is there an entry fee?

Regular nights have no cover charge, and drinks start at ¥500. Special events may have an admission fee. Please check the event announcement.

Where can I check tonight's opening hours?

Check Instagram @agayha_3f for closures and events. If anything is unclear, contact us before your visit.
